CareerCompass AI

Find the technical career that fits the skills you already have

Rate a few skills and CareerCompass AI ranks 13 engineering and software paths, explains each match and shows what to learn next.

AI-Based Career Guidance and Recommendation System Using Machine Learning. Guidance only, never a verdict on your future.

How it works

  1. Choose the areas that interest you.
  2. Rate your skills from 1 to 10. You only rate skills in your chosen areas.
  3. Get your top five paths with strengths, skill gaps, a learning path and project ideas.

This prototype scores you with a transparent weighted match against hand-written role profiles. The Flask backend and a Random Forest model trained on a clearly labelled synthetic dataset plug into this same page next.

Skill assessment

Pick the areas you’re curious about, then rate yourself honestly: 1 is “just heard of it”, 10 is “I could teach it”.
